# Staff access review

Organization owners review active and disabled memberships at least quarterly and immediately after employment/role changes. Export or inspect staff ID, display name, email, role, membership status, setup status, last login, practitioner status, identity synchronization flag, and administration history. Do not include patient data.

Confirm each active user is employed, has the least-privilege role, has completed setup/MFA policy, and still needs access. Disable stale accounts before provider cleanup. Confirm at least two organization administrators where staffing permits, investigate `identitySyncPending`, revoke sessions for elevated-risk changes, and retain signed review evidence under the approved audit-retention policy.
